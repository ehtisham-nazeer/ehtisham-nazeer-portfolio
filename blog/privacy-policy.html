Privacy Policy

LedgerLane (ehtishamnazeer.site/blog) is an independent publication hosted on Vercel.

Information we collect

We do not run user accounts and we do not collect names, addresses or payment details from readers. Like almost every website, standard server logs (including IP addresses and browser types) are processed by our hosting provider to operate and secure the site.

Cookies and advertising

This site may use cookies from Google services. When advertising (including Google AdSense) is active on this site, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via Google's Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com), and you can learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at google.com/policies/technologies/partner-sites.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which guides readers find useful. Analytics data is aggregated and is not used to identify individual readers.

External links

Our guides link to banks, software providers and regulators. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.
